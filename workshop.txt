title=[42.22.2] Fishing Spot Info
description=[h1]Fishing Spot Info[/h1]
description=In Build 42 every fish you catch makes that stretch of water poorer. The game keeps a hidden catch count for a 40x40 area around the spot, and the bite chance drops with it. This mod shows you, while you aim your rod, how well fish bite where your bobber will land, how many were caught in that area and when it restocks.
description=
description=[b]New in 1.2.0:[/b] the right-click "fishing spot" menu and window are gone. Everything now shows while you aim your rod, right next to the cursor, and higher Fishing levels colour more tiles around the aimed one.
description=
description=[h2]How to use[/h2]
description=Hold a fishing rod and aim (Ctrl). The tile where your bobber will land is coloured by how well fish bite there: [b]green[/b] best, [b]yellow-green[/b] good, [b]orange[/b] poor, [b]red[/b] almost nothing. A box right of the mouse cursor shows, live:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Spot score[/b]: bites per minute here compared with the best spot under the same weather and time.
description=[*][b]Fish here[/b]: the number the game uses for the bite chance, and its multiplier (x0.1 with no fish, x0.5 below 10, x1.0 up to 25, x1.5 above).
description=[*][b]Bite chance per try[/b] and its factors: fish, time of day, weather, temperature, hook. No hook on the rod, no bites.
description=[*][b]Slower tries[/b]: twice as slow near the shore, three times closer than 3 tiles to you.
description=[*][b]Area[/b]: fish caught in the 40x40 area around this spot and when it restocks (5 in-game days without a catch there). A fished area's border is drawn in orange.
description=[*][b]Dawn/dusk bonus[/b]: bites x1.2 from 04:00 to 07:00 and from 18:00 to 21:00, and when it starts or ends.
description=[/list]
description=With more Fishing skill you read more of the water: the tiles around the aimed one are coloured too, lighter, each by its own score. Levels 0-2: the aimed tile only. 3-5: plus the 4 next to it. 6-8: plus the 8 around it. 9-10: plus the 12 within 2 tiles.
description=Sweep the cursor over the water before you cast to find the green spots.
description=
description=[h2]How vanilla fishing depletes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Your first catch somewhere creates a 40x40 area centred on the bobber. Each catch inside it lowers every fish school in it by one.
description=[*]After 5 in-game days with no catch in the area, it goes back to full. The game applies the reset when fish are next counted there, so an area may show its old count until someone casts nearby.
description=[*]Fish schools move to new spots every day, but the catch count stays with the area. Moving out of the orange border is the quick fix.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=Read only: it never changes fishing, catches or the area counts. No vanilla files replaced. Safe to add to or remove from existing saves. Client-side; in multiplayer it shows what your client knows and has not been tested on a server.
description=
description=[h2]한국어[/h2]
description=Build 42에서는 물고기를 잡을수록 그 물가의 입질이 줄어듭니다. 게임은 잡은 자리 주변 40x40 구역마다 잡은 수를 몰래 세고, 그만큼 물고기 수와 입질 확률이 떨어집니다. 1.2.0부터 우클릭 메뉴와 창 대신 조준할 때 바로 보입니다. 낚싯대를 들고 조준(Ctrl)하면 찌가 떨어질 칸이 입질이 잘 되는 정도에 따라 색칠되고(초록 = 최고, 연두 = 좋음, 주황 = 나쁨, 빨강 = 거의 없음), 마우스 커서 오른쪽에 지점 점수, 그 칸의 물고기 수와 입질 배율, 한 번 시도할 때 입질 확률과 그 요소(물고기·시각·날씨·기온·바늘), 물가 근처나 3칸 이내라서 느려지는지, 그 구역에서 잡은 수와 회복까지 남은 시간(마지막으로 잡은 뒤 게임 시간 5일), 새벽/저녁 입질 보너스(04:00-07:00, 18:00-21:00에 x1.2)가 나옵니다. 낚시 레벨이 오르면 주변 물 칸도 각자 점수 색으로 옅게 칠해집니다(0-2: 조준한 칸만, 3-5: 상하좌우 4칸 더, 6-8: 둘레 8칸 더, 9-10: 2칸 거리 안 12칸 더). 던지기 전에 커서로 물 위를 훑어서 초록 칸을 찾으면 됩니다. 물고기 떼는 매일 옮겨 가지만 잡은 수는 구역에 남으니, 주황 테두리 밖으로 옮기면 됩니다. 조회만 하고 낚시 값은 바꾸지 않습니다. 기존 세이브에 넣고 빼도 됩니다.
