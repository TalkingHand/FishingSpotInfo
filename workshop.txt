title=Fishing Spot Info
description=[h1]Fishing Spot Info[/h1]
description=In Build 42 every fish you catch makes that stretch of water poorer. The game keeps a hidden catch count for a 40x40 area around the spot, and the bite chance drops with it. This mod shows you that count, how many fish are left and when the area restocks.
description=
description=[h2]How to use[/h2]
description=Right-click any water tile and choose [b]Fishing spot info[/b]. A small window shows, for that tile and live:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Fish here[/b]: the number the game uses for the bite chance, and the bite multiplier it gives (x0.1 with no fish, x0.5 below 10, x1.0 up to 25, x1.5 above).
description=[*][b]Caught in this area[/b]: fish caught in the 40x40 area around this spot, and the area's corners.
description=[*][b]Restocks in[/b]: the area resets after 5 in-game days without a catch there. Every catch restarts the timer.
description=[/list]
description=While the window is open, the tile is highlighted and the area's border is drawn on the ground: [b]green[/b] when the area is fully stocked, [b]orange[/b] when it has been fished. Close the window with X; right-click another water tile to move it there.
description=
description=[h2]How vanilla fishing depletes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Your first catch somewhere creates a 40x40 area centred on the bobber. Each catch inside it lowers every fish school in it by one.
description=[*]After 5 in-game days with no catch in the area, it goes back to full. The game applies the reset when fish are next counted there, so an area may show its old count until someone casts nearby.
description=[*]Fish schools move to new spots every day, but the catch count stays with the area. Moving out of the orange border is the quick fix.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=Read only: it never changes fishing, catches or the area counts. No vanilla files replaced. Safe to add to or remove from existing saves. Client-side; in multiplayer it shows what your client knows and has not been tested on a server.
description=
description=[h2]한국어[/h2]
description=Build 42에서는 물고기를 잡을수록 그 물가의 입질이 줄어듭니다. 게임은 잡은 자리 주변 40x40 구역마다 잡은 수를 몰래 세고, 그만큼 물고기 수와 입질 확률이 떨어집니다. 물 타일을 우클릭하고 [b]낚시터 정보[/b]를 고르면 그 칸의 물고기 수와 입질 배율, 그 구역에서 잡은 수, 회복까지 남은 시간(마지막으로 잡은 뒤 게임 시간 5일)이 창에 나옵니다. 창이 열려 있는 동안 바닥에 칸과 구역 테두리가 표시됩니다(초록 = 가득 참, 주황 = 줄어듦). 물고기 떼는 매일 옮겨 가지만 잡은 수는 구역에 남으니, 주황 테두리 밖으로 옮기면 됩니다. 조회만 하고 낚시 값은 바꾸지 않습니다. 기존 세이브에 넣고 빼도 됩니다.
